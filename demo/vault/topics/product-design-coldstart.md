# Coldstart's Founder-Centered Outreach Model

Coldstart is a tool that helps founders send personalized cold emails by combining AI-driven research and drafting with human oversight. The product emphasizes quality over quantity, using a structured approval process and learning from each interaction to improve future outreach.

## Key facts

- Coldstart's core idea is an agent that reads a founder's story and product, researches prospects, writes personal cold emails, and learns from every reply. [[arjun-s-core-idea-for-coldstart-is-an-agent-that-r--3c23f99c]]
- Arjun settled on a core loop where the agent researches and drafts overnight, the founder reviews an approval queue over coffee, and the agent sends and watches replies through the day. [[arjun-settled-on-a-core-loop-where-the-agent-resea--5b311b7e]]
- Every first-touch email in Coldstart requires human approval before sending to prevent the risks of automated outreach. [[arjun-made-sure-every-first-touch-email-in-coldsta--0afeddfd]]
- Arjun decided against a fully autonomous mode, believing that the product is about leverage with judgment, and that spam is a failure state. [[arjun-decided-against-a-fully-autonomous-mode-for--2f34b0ea]]
- Arjun's idea included an objection log where objections from replies persisted and quietly reshaped later drafts in the campaign. [[arjun-s-idea-was-an-objection-log-where-objections--abad8d94]]
- Coldstart's first version was scoped to five modules: prospect research, drafting, approval queue, reply triage, and campaign analytics. [[arjun-scoped-coldstart-s-first-version-to-five-mod--23aee1c1]]
- Arjun wrote the first draft of Coldstart's seed prompts, setting a plain, non-salesy tone that became the product's central promise. [[arjun-wrote-the-first-draft-of-coldstart-s-seed-pr--9c83ba4c]]
- A pilot with two founders revealed that the onboarding empty state failed to show what a good campaign looked like. [[arjun-ran-a-pilot-with-two-founders-and-noticed-th--332be415]]
- Arjun added a sample campaign walkthrough triggered from the empty state, allowing founders to request guidance without a forced tutorial. [[arjun-added-a-sample-campaign-walkthrough-triggere--76454c66]]
- Arjun restructured Coldstart's onboarding into three stages aligned with the domain warmup schedule, unlocking sending capacity as deliverability trust built. [[arjun-restructured-coldstart-s-onboarding-into-thr--0b312bf1]]
- Arjun connected the staged onboarding process to Marcus Webb's warmup infrastructure, ensuring pacing and deliverability supported each other. [[arjun-connected-the-staged-onboarding-process-to-m--329bc82f]]
- Arjun imagined a moment where a positive reply would shift the conversation to the founder, with the agent drafting a meeting proposal and stepping back. [[arjun-imagined-a-moment-where-a-positive-reply-fro--ac3cfd38]]
- Arjun rewrote Coldstart's seed prompts after the second pilot showed drafts becoming too salesy over long campaigns. [[arjun-rewrote-coldstart-s-seed-prompts-after-the-s--a5728ff0]]
- After the Claude API migration, a second pilot confirmed that the staged onboarding process was clearly understood by new founders. [[after-the-claude-api-migration-a-second-pilot-conf--f5f1e2eb]]
- Arjun hired a part-time copywriter to test the drafting voice used in Coldstart's approval queue. [[arjun-hired-a-part-time-copywriter-to-test-the-dra--c798d4e0]]
- Arjun decided the first booked meeting should happen within fourteen days of signup, aligning with the promise on Coldstart's landing page. [[arjun-decided-that-the-first-booked-meeting-should--83f92374]]
- Arjun added a plain-text email mode and full keyboard navigation after a pilot founder using a screen reader struggled with the approval queue. [[arjun-added-a-plain-text-email-mode-and-full-keybo--df307087]]
- Arjun finalized Coldstart's landing hero by using a live replay of a real campaign's approval queue instead of a static screenshot. [[arjun-finalized-coldstart-s-landing-hero-by-using--2982463e]]
- Arjun decided the public beta would cover only the first campaign flow, ending before multi-channel sequences, to keep the promise small and keepable. [[arjun-decided-the-public-beta-would-cover-only-the--f6c0c8d4]]
