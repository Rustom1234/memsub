# Lighthouse Keeper's Art Style Evolution

Maya transitioned Lighthouse Keeper's art style from pixel art to painterly gouache for richer mood expression, while maintaining simple character sprites for readability. She collaborated with illustrator Sofia Reyes to create a cohesive visual language across the game's environments.

## Key facts

- Maya chose a pixel-art style for Lighthouse Keeper to keep the project manageable as a solo artist. [[maya-chose-a-pixel-art-style-for-lighthouse-keeper--6733885b]]
- Maya mocked up the lighthouse tower interior in sixteen-by-sixteen pixel art but felt it failed to capture the moody coastal atmosphere she wanted. [[maya-mocked-up-the-lighthouse-tower-interior-in-si--d8d52d92]]
- Maya tried using painterly gouache-style backgrounds for the same lighthouse interior scene and found it better captured the desired mood. [[maya-tried-using-painterly-gouache-style-backgroun--0fff48a3]]
- Maya decided to switch Lighthouse Keeper's background art to a painterly gouache style, while keeping the simple flat-shaded character sprites for readability. [[maya-decided-to-switch-lighthouse-keeper-s-backgro--ec069d30]]
- Maya started looking for a background illustrator who could match the painterly gouche style for the game's five areas. [[maya-started-looking-for-a-background-illustrator--c3b3b6d6]]
- Maya read a chapter on gouache color theory, which influenced the style-guide discussion she had with Sofia Reyes later that week. [[maya-read-a-chapter-on-gouache-color-theory-which--f7ae4caa]]
- Maya hired Sofia Reyes, a freelance illustrator, to paint the background art for Lighthouse Keeper in the gouache style. [[maya-hired-sofia-reyes-a-freelance-illustrator-to--77a415dc]]
- Sofia Reyes delivered a color script that mapped each area's palette to the story's tide-based act structure. [[sofia-reyes-delivered-a-color-script-that-mapped-e--efe3e075]]
- Sofia Reyes finished the first background painting, the lighthouse tower interior, and Maya integrated it into the game build. [[sofia-reyes-finished-the-first-background-painting--d62d5a4e]]
- Maya finalized the keeper character's flat-shaded sprite sheet, ensuring the character remained simple against Sofia Reyes's detailed backgrounds. [[maya-finalized-the-keeper-character-s-flat-shaded--abb99c5f]]
- Maya and Sofia Reyes locked the final gouache palette to prevent any more area backgrounds from drifting in tone after the engine migration. [[maya-and-sofia-reyes-locked-the-final-gouache-pale--cb0a801f]]
- Maya re-imported Sofia Reyes's background paintings into the new Unity project during the engine migration, ensuring no quality loss. [[maya-re-imported-sofia-reyes-s-background-painting--d9dac6ad]]
- Maya designed Lighthouse Keeper's UI icons to match Sofia Reyes's painterly style instead of using a flat modern icon set. [[maya-designed-lighthouse-keeper-s-ui-icons-to-matc--0c98928b]]
- Maya added a dynamic lighting pass in Unity so the lighthouse beam sweeps across Sofia Reyes's painted backgrounds at night. [[maya-added-a-dynamic-lighting-pass-in-unity-so-the--2e42af85]]
- Sofia Reyes handled the tide-pools and cliffside-cave backgrounds, leaving the wrecked pier scene still to be completed. [[sofia-reyes-handled-the-tide-pools-and-cliffside-c--16042666]]
- Maya and Sofia Reyes created a short style guide that explains the gouache palette rules, making it easier for future contract artists to match the look. [[maya-and-sofia-reyes-created-a-short-style-guide-t--2fac2f7f]]
- Sofia Reyes finished the wrecked pier background, completing art for all five of Lighthouse Keeper's areas. [[sofia-reyes-finished-the-wrecked-pier-background-c--b152056b]]
