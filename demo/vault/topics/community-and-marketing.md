# Lighthouse Keeper's Development Journey

Maya developed Lighthouse Keeper through iterative updates, community engagement, and technical transitions. The project grew from a prototype to a polished demo with a dedicated audience.

## Key facts

- Maya published the first Lighthouse Keeper devlog post, introducing the premise and the early prototype. [[maya-published-the-first-lighthouse-keeper-devlog--4c9c357d]]
- Maya set up Lighthouse Keeper's Steam page with early screenshots from the prototype build. [[maya-set-up-lighthouse-keeper-s-steam-page-with-ea--f5eba20f]]
- After the devlog post was shared on a narrative-games forum, Lighthouse Keeper's Steam wishlist crossed one hundred adds. [[after-the-devlog-post-was-shared-on-a-narrative-ga--3a860a51]]
- Maya published a devlog explaining the switch from Godot to Unity, and it drew more comments than any previous post. [[maya-published-a-devlog-explaining-the-switch-from--2dba0043]]
- Maya opened a small Discord server for Lighthouse Keeper and answered questions directly from the first wishlisters. [[maya-opened-a-small-discord-server-for-lighthouse--efd73226]]
- May, updated the Lighthouse Keeper's Steam page screenshots to include Sofia Reyes's finished painterly backgrounds. [[maya-updated-the-lighthouse-keeper-s-steam-page-sc--15edff9f]]
- Maya put together a press kit featuring Sofia Reyes's background art and a short trailer cut from the Unity vertical slice. [[maya-put-together-a-press-kit-featuring-sofia-reye--85bb54d2]]
- The Lighthouse Keeper's Steam wishlist crossed five hundred adds after the new screenshots went up. [[the-lighthouse-keeper-s-steam-wishlist-crossed-fiv--6b5c60a9]]

- Maya applied to a small narrative-games showcase festival for a demo slot in the fall. [[maya-applied-to-a-small-narrative-games-showcase-f--3e4900ac]]

- Maya added an email newsletter signup to the Steam page a few weeks before the demo launch. [[maya-added-an-email-newsletter-signup-to-the-steam--43bdc25a]]
