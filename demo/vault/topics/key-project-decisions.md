# Maya's Lighthouse Keeper Journey

Maya developed Lighthouse Keeper as a solo project, balancing creative choices with practical considerations like tools, art style, and monetization. She adapted to changing circumstances, such as switching engines and adjusting her work schedule, while maintaining a focus on player experience and project manageability.

## Key facts

- Maya committed to waking up at six a.m. to get focused Lighthouse Keeper work done before her freelance calls started. [[maya-committed-to-waking-up-at-six-a-m-to-get-focu--f86e3bcf]]
- Maya decided to build Lighthouse Keeper in Godot 4, drawn by the free license, GDScript's Python-like syntax, and the lightweight editor. [[maya-decided-to-build-lighthouse-keeper-in-godot-4--c86a4f4f]]
- Maya chose a pixel-art style for Lighthouse Keeper because she wanted to keep the project manageable as a solo artist. [[maya-chose-a-pixel-art-style-for-lighthouse-keeper--6733885b]]
- Maya hired Priya Anand for ten hours a week of Godot contract work to help with the Lighthouse Keeper's save system. [[maya-hired-priya-anand-for-ten-hours-a-week-of-god--4b518c1c]]
- Maya decided to switch Lighthouse Keeper's background art to a painterly gouache style, while keeping the simple flat-shaded character sprites for readability. [[maya-decided-to-switch-lighthouse-keeper-s-backgro--ec069d30]]
- Maya settled on an eight a.m. wake time, which fit her natural rhythm and late-night debugging sessions better. [[maya-settled-on-an-eight-a-m-wake-time-which-fit-h--79533274]]
- Maya raised her hourly rate to eighty-five dollars, beginning with new contracts from Fintrack and Bramble and Co. [[maya-raised-her-hourly-rate-to-eighty-five-dollars--60645440]]
- Maya added an optional hint system that activates when players re-read the logbook, allowing those who want hints to request them without adjusting the difficulty setting. [[maya-added-an-optional-hint-system-that-activates--df676545]]
- Maya decided to switch Lighthouse Keeper from Godot to Unity after Unity's Indie Growth pricing change removed the runtime fee, and its asset store filled the dialogue-tooling gap. [[maya-decided-to-switch-lighthouse-keeper-from-godo--8049802d]]
- Maya raised her freelance rate to a hundred dollars an hour after Wren Callahan's referrals filled her calendar through the summer. [[maya-raised-her-freelance-rate-to-a-hundred-dollar--26f6e62d]]
- Maya decided the public demo would cover only the first act, ending right before the tide-pools reveal, to avoid spoiling the mystery. [[maya-decided-the-public-demo-would-cover-only-the--1e294628]]
- Maya decided to launch the public demo alongside a wishlist-focused devlog post covering only the first act. [[maya-decided-to-launch-the-public-demo-alongside-a--1c31af69]]
