# Arjun's Coldstart Development Journey

Arjun built Coldstart as a solo project, balancing consulting work and personal development. He shifted from a CLI tool to a web dashboard, refined his workflow, and adjusted his pricing strategy as the project evolved.

## Key facts

- Arjun committed to waking up at five a.m. to focus on Coldstart work before his consulting calls began. [[arjun-committed-to-waking-up-at-five-a-m-to-focus--67a7c07c]]
- Arjun chose to run Coldstart's inference on a self-hosted Qwen3 8B model, served by vLLM on a rented RTX 4090 server, motivated by the zero per-token cost, full data control, and his local-first instincts. [[arjun-chose-to-run-coldstart-s-inference-on-a-self--fd2ddec5]]
- Arjun initially decided Coldstart would ship as a CLI tool to keep the scope manageable for a solo engineer. [[arjun-initially-decided-coldstart-would-ship-as-a--de5539d6]]
- Arjun hired Marcus Webb for ten hours a week of contract work to help build Coldstart's deliverability infrastructure. [[arjun-hired-marcus-webb-for-ten-hours-a-week-of-co--7a4da6b2]]
- Arjun decided to switch Coldstart's interface to a web dashboard, keeping a thin CLI only for power users and scripting. [[arjun-decided-to-switch-coldstart-s-interface-to-a--5a0dc3f6]]
- Arjun settled on an eight a.m. wake time, which fit his natural rhythm and late-night debugging sessions better. [[arjun-settled-on-an-eight-a-m-wake-time-which-fit--5640ab7d]]
- Arjun raised his hourly consulting rate to $120, beginning with new contracts at Brightpath Legal and Corvid Analytics. [[arjun-raised-his-hourly-consulting-rate-to-120-beg--f0e6fd08]]
- Arjun added a sample campaign walkthrough triggered from the empty state, allowing founders to request guidance without being subjected to a forced tutorial. [[arjun-added-a-sample-campaign-walkthrough-triggere--76454c66]]
- Arjun decided to switch Coldstart's inference from the self-hosted Qwen3 stack to the Claude API after evaluations showed far fewer misread replies, and batch pricing plus prompt caching fixed the unit economics. [[arjun-decided-to-switch-coldstart-s-inference-from--497a99fe]]
- Arjun raised his consulting rate to $160 an hour after Omar Haddad's referrals filled his calendar through the summer. [[arjun-raised-his-consulting-rate-to-160-an-hour-af--aa3fd6ff]]
- Arjun decided the public beta would cover only the first campaign flow, ending before multi-channel sequences, to keep the promise small and keepable. [[arjun-decided-the-public-beta-would-cover-only-the--f6c0c8d4]]
- Arjun decided to launch the public beta at the same time as a devlog post that focused on the waitlist, covering only the first campaign flow. [[arjun-decided-to-launch-the-public-beta-at-the-sam--41170b7e]]
