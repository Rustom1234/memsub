# Coldstart Launch and Early Development

Coldstart began with a devlog post introducing its premise and a CLI prototype, leading to early user interest and a growing waitlist. The project evolved through feedback, design updates, and community engagement, eventually launching a public beta with a focused devlog.

## Key facts

- Arjun published the first Coldstart devlog post, introducing the premise and the early CLI prototype. [[arjun-published-the-first-coldstart-devlog-post-in--b39b82d4]]
- Arjun created Coldstart's waitlist landing page using early screenshots from the prototype dashboard. [[arjun-created-coldstart-s-waitlist-landing-page-us--efd2ba98]]
- After the devlog post was shared on an indie-hackers forum, Coldstart's waitlist crossed one hundred signups. [[after-the-devlog-post-was-shared-on-an-indie-hacke--b4f3e45c]]
- Arjun published a devlog explaining the switch from the self-hosted Qwen3 stack to the Claude API, and it received more comments than any previous post. [[arjun-published-a-devlog-explaining-the-switch-fro--731831a1]]
- Arjun opened a small Discord server for Coldstart and answered questions directly from the first waitlisters. [[arjun-opened-a-small-discord-server-for-coldstart--8a4dc5fc]]
- Arjun updated Coldstart's landing page screenshots to showcase Lena Petrova's finished dashboard design. [[arjun-updated-coldstart-s-landing-page-screenshots--cd22e0c5]]
- Arjun put together a launch kit that included Lena Petrova's dashboard screens and a short demo video he cut from a live campaign run. [[arjun-put-together-a-launch-kit-that-included-lena--b7ef1ff8]]
- After the new screenshots went up, Coldstart's waitlist crossed five hundred signups. [[after-the-new-screenshots-went-up-coldstart-s-wait--ec067de3]]
- Arjun shared the demo video with a small feedback Discord channel and revised the pacing of the opening shot. [[arjun-shared-the-demo-video-with-a-small-feedback--244f4416]]
- Arjun applied to a fall AI-product showcase for a live demo slot for Coldstart. [[arjun-applied-to-a-fall-ai-product-showcase-for-a--4b9442c5]]
- Arjun decided to launch the public beta at the same time as a devlog post that focused on the waitlist, covering only the first campaign flow. [[arjun-decided-to-launch-the-public-beta-at-the-sam--41170b7e]]
- Arjun added an email newsletter signup to the landing page just before the beta launch. [[arjun-added-an-email-newsletter-signup-to-the-land--05717f6b]]
