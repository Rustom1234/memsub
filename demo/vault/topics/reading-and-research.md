# Maya's Design Inspiration Sources

Maya draws inspiration from a variety of sources to shape the design of Lighthouse Keeper, including game design theory, sound design principles, narrative techniques, and indie dev strategies.

## Key facts

- Maya used *The Art of Game Design* to gather ideas for the puzzles in Lighthouse Keeper. [[maya-picked-up-the-art-of-game-design-by-jesse-sch--24affab2]]
- Maya bookmarked the Godot documentation's TileMap and Resource sections for ongoing reference. [[maya-bookmarked-the-godot-documentation-s-tilemap--5e46a979]]
- Maya applied Jesse Schell's 'lens of the problem' to the tide-pools puzzle after playtesting. [[maya-decided-to-apply-jesse-schell-s-lens-of-the-p--dc00fb12]]
- Maya studied Braid's puzzle design to understand how mechanics could be introduced without tutorials. [[maya-read-a-postmortem-on-braid-s-puzzle-design-an--d3b4bfa1]]
- Maya noted that irregular rhythms in sound design can more effectively engage players. [[maya-read-a-sound-design-chapter-that-argued-playe--8eb7b31d]]
- Maya followed a narrative-design newsletter for environmental storytelling techniques. [[maya-subscribed-to-a-narrative-design-newsletter-t--d859ce04]]
- Maya was influenced by Gone Home's use of found documents for Lighthouse Keeper's letter-based mystery. [[maya-read-an-analysis-of-gone-home-s-use-of-found--c4e6e21a]]
- Maya analyzed What Remains of Edith Finch to learn how to pace a letter-driven mystery. [[maya-played-through-what-remains-of-edith-finch-to--c5219401]]
- Maya wanted to use Edith Finch's control-changing trick for logbook flashback scenes. [[maya-wanted-to-borrow-edith-finch-s-trick-of-chang--2783af13]]
- Maya started working through Unity's C# scripting documentation for engine migration. [[maya-began-working-through-unity-s-official-c-scri--57974379]]
- Maya read about Firewatch's pacing to match the lighthouse's isolated tone. [[maya-read-an-analysis-of-firewatch-s-isolated-sett--440535f2]]
- Maya used a chapter on ambient sound design to guide her audio contractor. [[maya-read-a-chapter-on-ambient-sound-design-in-gam--227cd674]]
- Maya began reading a marketing book for solo indie developers focused on wishlist growth. [[maya-started-reading-a-book-on-marketing-for-solo--dfeb12c6]]
- Maya imagined a post-credits scene where recovered letters are displayed in a museum. [[maya-imagined-a-post-credits-scene-where-the-recov--1c21c82f]]
- Maya started listening to a podcast about pricing a first commercial release. [[maya-started-listening-to-a-solo-indie-dev-podcast--88b0e1ce]]
